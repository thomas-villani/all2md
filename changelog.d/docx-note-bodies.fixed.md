- DOCX footnotes and endnotes keep their formatting, tabs, links and lists. Every note paragraph was read as plain text, so `**bold**` in a note printed as `bold`, and the space Word puts after the note number printed as a double space (`[^1]:  text`). Tracked changes, content controls and fields inside notes are now resolved too: before, `revisions="reject"` still printed text inserted in a note, and `revisions="mark"` did not mark it.
