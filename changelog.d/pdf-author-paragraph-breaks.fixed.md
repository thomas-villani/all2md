- **PDF paragraphs no longer run into their neighbors when a document sets no space between
  them.** Many Word documents mark a paragraph's end only with an empty line, a bullet, or a
  line that stops short, and the text on either side used to run together into a single
  paragraph. An empty line (or a bullet whose glyph extracts as a space) now ends the paragraph.
  So does a line that stopped early enough for the next line's first word to have fitted after
  it, since Word only wraps when the next word does not fit. On 755 Word documents from
  elsewhere, made into PDFs by Word, the share of paragraphs kept whole rose from 26% to 57%.
  A line ended with a manual line break now becomes its own paragraph, which looks the same.
