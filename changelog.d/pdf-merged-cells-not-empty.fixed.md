- PDF: a bordered table whose rows are cut at different column widths, or that has merged cells, is no longer thrown away as "mostly empty". PyMuPDF's table finder leaves a gap wherever a row has no cell of its own, and the empty-cell guard counted those gaps as empty cells, so registers, schedules and forms from Word lost their tables and came out as run-together text. A grid with text in most of its rows is now judged on its real cells only; charts and frames around a page's running head are still rejected.
